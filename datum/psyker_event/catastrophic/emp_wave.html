emp_wave
/datum/psyker_event/catastrophic/emp_wave

Emits a series of EMP waves from a synthetic Psyker.

Vars

maximum_pulsesMaximum number of EMP pulses in the sequence.
minimum_pulsesMinimum number of EMP pulses in the sequence.
overload_overlayOverlay displayed during the delay before the first pulse.
overloading_psykerPsyker currently displaying the overload warning overlay.
pulse_radiusRadius of the square affected by each pulse. A radius of two produces a 5x5 area.
pulses_remainingPulses remaining in the current sequence.

Procs

begin_pulsesClears the overload warning and starts the EMP sequence.
clear_overload_overlayRemoves the warning overlay from the Psyker.
pulseEmits one pulse and schedules the next at a random delay, deleting the event after the final pulse.
